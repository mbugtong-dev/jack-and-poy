GAME MECHANICS

Basic Rules

Rock beats Scissors.

Paper beats Rock.

Scissors beats Paper.

Players choose their moves simultaneously.

Scoring System

Win: +10 accumulated points.

Draw: +5 accumulated points for both players.

Lose: 0 accumulated points.

Match points are awarded for each round won.

The first player to reach 5 match points must have a lead of at least 2 points to win the match.

At 4–4, the match continues. A round win makes the score 5–4, but the player must win another round to finish the match.

If the score becomes 5–5, the match continues until one player leads by 2 points.

Streak System

- Each consecutive round win increases the winner's streak by 1.

- When a player loses, their streak resets to 0.

- During a draw, both players keep their current streaks. Neither streak increases nor resets.

- The same streak rules apply to both the player and the computer.

Special Abilities

- Three consecutive wins unlock a random special ability. Additional abilities can be unlocked at 6, 9, 12, and so on, provided no ability is currently waiting to be used.

- Unlocking an ability does not reset the player's streak.

Kandado

Allows the player to lock one move, preventing the computer from choosing that move during the round.

Bawi

Allows the player to change their selected move during the 2-second Bawi window after the moves are revealed.

Baligtaran

Reverses the normal winning rules for one round.

Peek

- Peek becomes available after reaching a streak of 2 consecutive wins.

- Each use costs 20 accumulated points.

- It reveals two possible computer moves. One is the computer's actual move, and the other is a decoy.

- The player must choose which move to use based on the revealed information.

- Peek can be used once per round.

- A draw preserves the streak and keeps Peek unlocked. Losing resets the streak and locks Peek again.
